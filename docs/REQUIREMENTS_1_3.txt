B. HISTORY – GROUP ACTIVITY INTO REAL STUDY SESSIONS
==================================================

The current History screen is showing individual tracking fragments such as:

Wi-Fi      2h 36m
Manual     0h 3m
Manual     0h 0m
Wi-Fi      0h 15m

This makes the History screen confusing.

I want StudyNest to introduce the concept of a REAL STUDY SESSION.

Example:

Suppose I arrive at the library and study for approximately 3 hours.

During those 3 hours there may be:
- Wi-Fi disconnections/reconnections,
- manual tracking,
- short breaks,
- automatic tracking events,
- temporary interruptions.

These internal tracking records should NOT automatically appear as four or five
separate main sessions.

Instead, History should show something like:

SESSION 1
29 September
Total Study Time: 3h 00m
Start: 5:46 PM
End: 9:21 PM

The session may internally contain:

• Wi-Fi tracked period
• Manual tracked period
• Breaks
• reconnections

but the user should primarily see ONE study session.

If I leave and later return for another genuinely separate period of studying,
show:

SESSION 2
29 September
Total Study Time: 2h 10m

Therefore a single date may contain:

29 September
Total: 5h 10m

Session 1 — 3h 00m
Session 2 — 2h 10m

IMPORTANT:
Do not simply combine every record from the same day.

There must still be separate sessions when the user genuinely finishes one
study period and later begins another.

Design sensible session-grouping logic based on the app's existing tracking
architecture.

Short Wi-Fi interruptions/reconnections should not unnecessarily create new
sessions.

Break time must continue to be excluded from actual study duration.

Zero-duration records such as:

Manual
0h 0m
6:05 PM – 6:05 PM

should NOT clutter the normal History interface.

Either prevent meaningless zero-duration sessions from being stored or filter
them from the user-visible history, whichever is safer for the existing
architecture.
