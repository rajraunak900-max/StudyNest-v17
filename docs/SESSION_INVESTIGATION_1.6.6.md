## What was inspected

- FocusRecorder.sync inserts one LibraryVisit when combined Wi-Fi/geofence presence becomes true, with start, elapsed/boot timestamps and minimumMinutes copied from settings at arrival.
- The open row has end=null and qualified=null. Ordinary synchronization updates its checkpoint; it does not finalize qualification at the threshold.
- VisitQualification.finish updates that same row on closure; qualification uses continuous end-start >= captured minimumMinutes*60,000. Breaks, distraction and Actual Study do not control qualification.
- VisitQualification.forDay selects qualified=true, end!=null rows overlapping the requested local date, distinct by visit ID. It excludes ALL active visits even after one hour. HistoryScreen uses that query for its completed library session list. The existing 1.6.5 test pendingVisitIsNotACompletedSession explicitly expects null qualification/end after 15 minutes.
- SessionEngine inserts the underlying automatic StudySession immediately, and same-day departure/re-entry may reuse that timer identity by design. LibraryVisit identities are separate; closing a visit then returning inserts a new visit. Completed visit qualification must not be confused with the broader timer identity.
- confirmDeparture closes the LibraryVisit at the persisted exitAt timestamp after 90 seconds' departure grace. Combined Wi-Fi/geofence presence prevents closure while either enabled source still says inside. A brief interruption before grace does not close the visit.
- Manual timer sessions retain their existing StudyPeriods presentation. New visit coverage excludes overlapping periods to avoid duplicate/under-threshold session cards.
- Process-start recoverVisit closes an unobserved open visit at its persisted checkpoint. Recovery deliberately excludes unobserved time; it does not infer uninterrupted presence through process death. This limitation needs explicit tests/reporting, not a promise of exact phone recovery.

The phone database has not been accessed, so stale state or device-specific background problems cannot be ruled out from the observation alone.
