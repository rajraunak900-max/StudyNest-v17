# StudyNest 1.6.9 physical acceptance checklist

These are physical tests to perform on your own phone; Codex's automated/Robolectric results do not prove hardware behaviour. Android/OEM deep idle, restricted apps, force-stop, permission denial and channel settings can prevent or delay execution. No exact scheduling guarantee under every power state.

1. Install the signed 1.6.9 APK directly over 1.6.8. Do not uninstall or clear app data. Compare saved Wi-Fi networks, History, tasks, goals/streaks, monitoring selections and settings before/after. Schema is unchanged.
2. In App Monitoring type wha, inst, face, you for installed apps. Verify case-insensitivity and visible-name ranking. Select while filtering, clear/re-enter query, confirm unsaved changes persist until explicit Save, then reopen and confirm saved selections. Check system-app toggle/master toggle/Usage Access.
3. Check Efficiency percentage only: 49.9 red, 50.0 amber, 74.9 amber, 75.0 green, in Light and Dark. Cards/icons/layout unchanged; unknown is neutral.
4. Open History's Week & Month Report and switch both periods. Confirm five useful metric cards remain and duplicate upper Tracked study/lower All-time tracked/Daily average/Best streak and redundant explanation are absent. Graph/Calendar and goals remain unchanged.
5. Make two same-day library visits. Verify notification Library and Actual equal today's History Library/Actual totals, including previous visits. They are daily library metrics, not the current timer or outside manual-study total. Compare distraction, manual pause, stopped timer and pending away states.
6. Confirm 90-second departure/sound action remains independent of 30-minute grace, clocks freeze at the original disconnect cutoff, Efficiency stays fixed outside, and no grace time inflates Library/Actual/Break/Distraction. Check reconnect within grace retains the same visit; legitimate geofence presence still keeps Library accounting active if enabled.
7. Pause automatic study inside, wait 20 minutes, leave and stay away >30 minutes. Check old visit is completed/qualified normally, old Break remains20 minutes, then return: new visit and fresh automatic timer start, current Break zero. Check the old reminder never fires.
8. Pause again during the new visit. This is a fresh Break starting at zero. Keep phone locked through 45 minutes: observe one approximately 2-second vibration and one Break Reminder explaining45 minutes. Keep paused through 60/90 minutes: no repeat. Tap reminder: Home opens; tracking notification remains separate.
9. Resume and pause again: a new 45-minute eligibility. Separately leave before 45 minutes, stay outside past that deadline, then return: no stale vibration/notification. If notification permission/channel is disabled, delivery is blocked; the app does not repeatedly retry that break.
10. Across local midnight, compare notification's new-day totals with History, with one ongoing visit and pending away. Existing reconciliation refreshes on the established service/event schedule; no new midnight alarm was added. Confirm no yesterday total after reconciliation.
11. Check normal battery use with locked/screen-off monitoring and detached power; test default and OEM-restricted/deep-idle conditions separately. No physical battery measurement by Codex. Keep 1.6.8 artifacts as a rollback reference; Android may reject installing an older versionCode without special developer measures—do not uninstall to roll back if preserving data.
