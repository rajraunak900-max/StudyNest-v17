Confirmed departure freezes Library/Actual/Efficiency at ORIGINAL disconnect cutoff, excluding provisional confirmation time; sound uses separate 90-second Wi-Fi timer. No fake Break/Distraction during away. Existing short first-deadline wake protection at most 100 seconds and cancellation/release remain unchanged.

Wi-Fi automatic grace remains 30 minutes from combined exitAt. Within grace: same visit ID/original start; persisted awayRanges excludes the entire confirmed-away interval; automatic departure-paused timers resume, USER-paused timers stay paused; the old Break already closed on presence loss and no new Break is inferred on return. Library and Actual resume normally subject to monitoring; no new Break until a new explicit Pause. Explicit Manual timers and ordinary automatic departure-paused timer identity remain baseline behaviour. Location-only finalization rules remain unchanged.
