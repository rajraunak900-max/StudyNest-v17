## Confirmed code defects and narrow corrections

1. **Late departure confirmation followed by re-entry:** same-day arrival could clear an expired persisted departure before a delayed tick finalized the old visit, merging two genuine visits. The regression first produced one visit where two were required. SessionEngine.signal now confirms the old expired departure before applying the new presence event, inside its existing transaction. Grace and combined presence behavior remain intact.
2. **Process restart during departure grace:** recovery closed the old visit at a checkpoint after actual departure, while grace alone could create a phantom new visit. A regression first produced two records instead of one and risked classifying 9:59 as qualifying. FocusRecorder.recoverVisit now caps recovery at the earlier checkpoint/known departure; sync permits new rows only from detected presence. Grace can preserve an existing row but cannot prove another arrival.

Both behaviors existed before1.6.5. The new1.6.5 minimum filter can expose existing fragmentation/unfinished-record behavior. Qualification remains continuous end-start >= the threshold captured at arrival; pauses/distractions are not subtracted. No migration, polling, additional jobs/scans/GPS/UsageStats work or wake locks were added. Existing conservative recovery does not fabricate time while a process was dead.
