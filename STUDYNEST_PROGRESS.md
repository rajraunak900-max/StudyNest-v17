## Session lifecycle, departure and minimum duration

Legitimate presence is enabled Wi-Fi OR enabled geofence. Library Time continues inside while distracted or manually paused. The working ~90-second confirmation and ~30-minute visit continuity grace are independent. At confirmed departure, study pauses and sound restoration follows its existing independent Wi-Fi policy; the visit remains pending. Accounting is frozen at the original disconnect cutoff, excluding the confirmed-away interval. Brief under-90-second disconnect tolerance stays unchanged.

Reconnecting within 30 minutes continues the same visit ID/start and excludes the awayRanges gap. Departure-paused automatic tracking resumes, while a user-paused timer stays paused. Manual Break ends on loss of presence; returning never invents a new Break. At the full boundary, the old visit finalizes; a late arrival reconciles pending departure first. No duplicate session or grace-period inflation is introduced.

Stable 1.6.9's manually paused automatic timer expiry remains: after full Wi-Fi grace a user-paused automatic timer ends at the original cutoff, and a later arrival creates a fresh timer/visit with no carried-over Break. Explicit Manual timers and ordinary departure-paused automatic timers retain their existing distinct rules. Sound timers, location-only rules and background scheduling are unchanged.

Visit qualification uses accumulated legitimate presence, excluding confirmed gaps, against the minimum duration captured on arrival (default 10 minutes, configurable 1–1440). Short visits contribute totals but do not become qualifying session cards. Existing qualified and legacy nullable-threshold records remain unchanged. Midnight/local time and DST split daily accounting without duplicating an underlying visit; away-only dates earn no presence. Process recovery does not invent unobserved focus or arrival.
